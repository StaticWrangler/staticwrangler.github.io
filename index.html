04 / A little help

Let's get you
back to watching.

Start with the common questions here. Our dedicated support thread on the EPGenius Discord will be linked when it's available.

Community support thread coming soon

When reporting a problem, share the channel, matchup, date, time zone and player. Keep credentials and full playlist or guide links private.

Is StaticWrangler a TV subscription?+

No. It adds curation and guide information to provider access you already have. You'll need your own compatible subscription and player.

How do I get the beta?+

The T-Rex and Dream 4K editions are still being prepared for a free beta. This site will share release information and setup guidance when they're ready.

Will every game be available?+

Coverage depends on your provider, published event feeds and available schedules. StaticWrangler cannot add a broadcast your subscription doesn't carry. Some feeds come on air only close to kickoff, and exhibition schedules may be incomplete.

Why isn't a game live yet?+

A listing can appear ahead of the event so you can find it later. The provider may show an off-air screen until near the start. Check the listed start time and your player's time-zone setting.

The guide or artwork looks out of date.+

Try your player's playlist and guide refresh options. Some players also cache artwork. If it persists, note the channel and the programme shown, plus the time and player, for the support thread.

Does a 4K option mean every game is native 4K?+

No. Feed resolution and the original production format can differ. 4K options are included when a suitable feed is available; quality and availability can vary by channel and event.

Is there a charge?+

The launch is planned as a free beta. Optional donations may come later. Your provider subscription remains separate.
